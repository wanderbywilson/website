Questions

The things clients
most often ask.

What is the benefit of working with a travel advisor? +

Travel advisors bring first-hand knowledge, a high-touch experience, unmatched connections around the world, and in-destination support. Technology has made some bookings easier, but it can’t replace lived experience, relationships with the right hotels, and someone advocating for you in real time when plans shift.

Do you get better rates than if I booked myself? +

Sometimes we have access to negotiated rates, but the real value is in saving you time, stress, and frustration — before and during travel — while adding meaningful perks at the hotels you’d already book anyway.

Why do you provide bundled package rates? +

We focus on the whole trip, not individual components. Bundling accommodations, transportation, activities, and details together lets us leverage our industry relationships to secure the best overall value while keeping the itinerary seamless.

Do you have a trip minimum? Recommended budgets? +

We specialize in high-end hotels and custom itineraries. Most properties we partner with start around $600 / night before taxes, and full-service itineraries typically start at $15,000 / week for two (excluding international flights). For fully customized trips:

· Europe: from $15,000 / week, excluding international flights
· Africa & Safari: from $20,000 for a 10-night luxury safari, excluding international flights
· Overwater bungalows: from $15,000 for 8 nights, excluding international flights
