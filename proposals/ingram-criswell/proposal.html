Slow Arrival, Tuscan Dusk

Touch down at Rome Fiumicino, where your English-speaking driver will be waiting at arrivals. Settle into a Mercedes sedan for the scenic three-and-a-half-hour drive north into Tuscany.

Check in to Hotel Calzaiuoli, your home for the next four nights — a refined four-star tucked between the Duomo and Piazza della Signoria. The evening is yours to wander.

Private FCO transfer
Wednesday
28

Florence

David, the Duomo, and a Glass of Chianti by Noon

Meet your private licensed guide in the lobby for a half-day on foot through the city that birthed the Renaissance. Begin at Piazza della Repubblica, once the site of the Roman forum, then cross to Piazza del Duomo where your guide will tell you all about its Gothic bell-tower, Romanesque baptistery, and the great cathedral itself.

Continue to the Mercato Centrale to sample Tuscany: focaccia, prosciutto and pecorino, a glass of Chianti — and, if the spirit moves you, the local lampredotto. Cap the morning at the Accademia with reserved entry to stand before Michelangelo’s David. The afternoon is yours.

Private guide · David reserved entry

Two Days At Leisure

By design: nothing on the schedule. The most common note I get back from clients is that the unscheduled days are the ones they remember. Florence in late October rewards aimlessness — a stroll through the Oltrano artisan district, leather shopping, a long lunch you didn’t book, the Brancacci frescoes when the school groups thin out at three.

Rome

The Colosseum Before the Crowds

Meet your private licensed guide at the Arch of Constantine for a half-day in the city’s deepest layer. Begin inside the Colosseum, walking the arena where Rome stage-managed its empire; continue through the Roman Forum, once the social, political, and commercial heart of the world; and finish at the Circus Maximus, the vast chariot-racing valley still etched into the city’s grain.

All reserved entries are included. The afternoon is yours — a long lunch in Monti, a walk to the Pantheon, an evening passeggiata through Trastevere.

Private guide · reserved admissions
