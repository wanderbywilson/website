Questions

The things people
most often ask.

Does it cost more to book through Wander by Wilson? +

No. You pay the same rate as booking direct with the cruise line — often with onboard credits, suite upgrades, or hosted experiences layered on top.

Can you help with flights and hotels too? +

Yes. We coordinate the whole trip — flights, pre/post-cruise hotel stays, private transfers, and shore excursions. One advisor, one itinerary.

Do you charge planning fees for cruises? +

Cruise bookings are complimentary. For more involved itineraries — multi-stop land programs, custom private touring, or large-group logistics — we may quote a planning fee. We’ll always be transparent about it up front.

Can you help choose between cruise lines? +

Absolutely. That’s where we add the most value — matching your travel style, preferred regions, and who’s coming to the line, ship, cabin, and itinerary that actually fits.

Do you only book luxury cruises? +

Primarily, yes. We focus on the cruise lines where our preferred-partner status unlocks meaningful perks for clients.
